THROUGH THE SUBWAY is a conceptual online interpretation of UNDER THE SURFACE, a book documenting the subway tiles of New York City.

It combines the geometric forms of square tiles with a stylized structure of a subway system. This website turns each square “train” into... users travel through the different stations, they encounter four stories from the original book
